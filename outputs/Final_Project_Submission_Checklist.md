1. Open the Role Explorer.
2. Select LeBron James, `2020 LAL`.
3. Select James Harden, `2025 LAC`.
4. Show the comparison insight, role map, fingerprint, and closest matches.
5. State that the result is descriptive and not a player ranking.
6. Open Team Roster Lab.
7. Choose Oklahoma City.
8. Select one player in the roster map.
9. Switch to Coverage and point to the benchmark.
10. Add Boston as the comparison team.
11. Use **Swap teams** to show that the selected and comparison teams can exchange places without rebuilding the comparison.
12. State that the top 10 by minutes are a rotation proxy, not an official depth chart.
